Bracelets

/collections/bracelets

My jewelry line started with one bracelet, the Broken Horse bracelet I designed and created during a silversmith class. Each piece I make is handmade and one of a kind. Broken Horse Bracelet | Sterling Silver is a new improved design, still hand stamped. I use sterling silver that is 1/2 in wide and 18 gauge. The bracelet is slightly adjustable.

The Rosarita Bracelet sets a rich red cabochon on a hand hammered sterling silver band. Rosarita is glass slag, a by-product of gold refining. Rosarita Stud Earrings are in Earrings. The Royston Rectangle Bracelet is handmade in Litchfield, Nebraska, with Royston turquoise in green hues with hints of brown matrix. Wear it in a stack or on its own.

Earrings

/collections/earrings

Campitos Stud Earrings are a go to pair in blue Campitos turquoise with a hint of matrix. The stones are set in fine silver with a tiny twist wire border, and the pair is stamped sterling and with my maker's mark. Golden Hills Stud Earrings are everyday studs in Golden Hills turquoise, vibrant blues with brown matrix, approximately .75 x .50 inches.

Palomino Variscite Stud Earrings have a soft yellow stone with hints of brown and a touch of green, set in fine silver and accented with sterling silver raindrops. They dress up or dress down. For something bigger, look in Bracelets or Necklaces.
