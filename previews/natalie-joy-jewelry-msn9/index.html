Hoops

/collections/hoops

Several of the hoops here are lightweight and easy to wear. Ric Rac Hoops take the ric rac details from vintage clothes and put them on your ears, hanging 0.75" in cast brass on silver posts. Landline Hoops are the old kitchen phone cord as a hoop, 1 1/4" across, with silver posts and cast brass.

For the big hoop lovers, Carousel Hoops are a more dramatic version of Loop De Hoops: 1 5/8" across, lightweight and playful. Every order is handmade, so please allow 7-10 days after you order for your pair to reach you. If you are dressing for a party, the Statement Earrings are the next stop.

Statement Earrings

/collections/statement-earrings

Some of the pairs here are lightweight statement earrings. After studying and creating small-scale sculptures, I eventually found a way to make sculpture you can wear. Each pair is hand fabricated in our Portland studio, and it shows in the tiny details.

Spaghetti Earrings are a wiggly pair that hangs 2 7/8" long in cast brass, an easy way to dress up a party look. Party Earrings are named for a reason: they are ready for a party, 3/4" long and 5/8" wide. Hilma Earrings hang 2" long in cast brass, with a silver post. If you would rather have a hoop, Fleur Hoops and Cameo Hoops are in Hoops.
