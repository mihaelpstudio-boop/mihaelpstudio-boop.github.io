Studs

/collections/studs

Small earrings for every day, handmade, from start to finish, in Jaci's home studio just outside of Toledo, Ohio. Depending on the pair, the metal is sterling silver or 14k gold fill, gold vermeil or brass.

Kitten Tooth Stud Earrings are cast from a baby tooth of Jaci's kitten, Beatrix, in sterling silver, approximately 5mm wide. Gold Vermeil Shark Tooth Earrings are cast from a tooth Jaci found at Caspersen Beach in Venice, FL, then plated in 14k gold.

Tiny Studs with Chain Ear Jacket in 14k Gold Fill or Sterling Silver come with a plain pair of backs, to wear with or without the chain. Hoops and all the rest are in Earrings.

Earrings

/collections/earrings

Hoops and studs, every pair handmade, from start to finish, and subtle enough for every day. The hoops are substantial in appearance, but easy on the ears.

Big Hammered Hoops are 2" in diameter, 14 gauge wire in sterling silver or 14k gold fill. Dash Hoops are just over 1 1/2", each little line hammered individually. Daisy Hoops are 18mm across, in sterling silver or gold vermeil, with the tiniest little daisies. All of these are lead and nickel free.

Dash Hoops and Big Hammered Hoops are made to order, so allow one week to ship. The smallest pairs, to wear alone or stacked up with other earrings, are in Studs.
