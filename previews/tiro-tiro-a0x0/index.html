RINGS

/collections/rings

These are rings for everyday wear, in solid brass or sterling silver and in solid gold. The Ilse Ring is a contoured knife edge band, .25" wide, with curved side cutouts for comfortable wear on its own or in a stack. The Lou Ring, our contoured take on the cigar band, is .5" wide, so we recommend sizing up.

The Cirrus Ring in Gold, a staple of ours for years, comes in solid 10k or 14k gold. Ask us about fitting it around a solitaire for a wedding set. Each of these is made to order in sizes 4-12 and ships in 2-4 weeks. Need one sooner? Email us before ordering.

EARRINGS

/collections/earrings

Our earrings are sculptural pieces for everyday wear: studs set with stone spheres, and modular hoops that take removable drops. The Anni Earrings in Carnelian are sculpted circular studs holding bright carnelian spheres, in solid brass or silver. They weigh 3 grams each. The Anni Earrings in Violet Onyx swap in cheery onyx spheres. Both are made to order and ship in 2-4 weeks.

If you would rather not wait, the modular hoops marked Ready to Ship go out within 3-5 days. Cast in solid brass or sterling silver, they reach 1.5" long with the drops on. More pieces already made are in Ready to Ship.
