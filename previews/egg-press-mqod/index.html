all single cards

/collections/all-cards

Every card here is letterpress printed in our Portland, Oregon studio. We have been making our cheeky and thoughtful greeting cards there since 1999. Our mid-century Heidelberg windmill presses do the work, and every color is a hand-mixed ink with its own run through the press.

Monster Hug Sympathy Letterpress Greeting Card is a six-arm hug for someone who needs one. Party Cheetah Birthday Letterpress Greeting Card sends a little party cheetah to your favorite person. Toadally Awesome Birthday Letterpress Greeting Card is a happy birthday and a toad, happily side-eyeing. If you are planning for next year, our 2027 calendars are in Calendars.

Calendars

/collections/calendars

These are our 2027 calendars, plus posters for keeping track of things. The 2027 Egg Press Letterpress Postcard Wall Calendar comes as 13 tear-away art postcards, spiral bound and letterpress printed in Portland, OR. When a month ends, tear off its art and mail it to a friend with a postcard stamp.

Chicken 2027 Calendar Illustrated Risograph Print is a calendar you can read at a glance, with a cheery spring chicken. Zodiac Signs Illustrated Risograph Print is a cheeky reference poster with each sign's dates and traits, so, hopefully, you never forget a birthday again. For everything else you send, the cards are in all single cards.
