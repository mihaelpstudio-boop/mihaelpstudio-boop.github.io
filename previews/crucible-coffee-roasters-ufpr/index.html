Single Origin

/collections/single-origin

This is where the single origin coffees live.

Costa Rica Tarrazú Tirra is a light roast, natural process coffee from the CoopeTarrazú cooperative, grown at 1,450 - 1,500 masl, with mulberry wine and chocolate glaze. Ethiopia Kuru Konga is a washed, medium-light roast from smallholder producers in the Yirgacheffe district, near the village of Konga, grown at 2,000 - 2,300 masl, with yellow peach and macadamia nut. Brazil Cerrado is a medium-dark natural from the Cerrado region of Minas Gerais, grown in rich Terra Roxa soil, with dark chocolate and walnut. If you would rather start with a blend, those are in Blends.

Blends

/collections/blends

Our roastmaster created Mountain Music to bridge the gap between an everyday nutty, chocolatey cup and a complex single origin. It is a medium roast from South America and Africa, black cherry, cocoa and hazelnut, made for bluegrass Sunday mornings on the porch.

Iron Horse is a medium roast from Central America, bright citrus and rich chocolate, strong and easy-drinking for your 9 to 5. Before The Dawn is a dark roast, dark chocolate with robust, smoky undertones, the aroma of a classic dark roast that recalls a first sip of coffee from childhood. If you would rather taste one origin at a time, those are in Single Origin.
