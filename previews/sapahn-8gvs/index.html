All Sapahn

/collections/all-sapahn-products

This is all of Sapahn in one place. Our bags are built to last and crafted by highly-skilled Thai female artisans, with care down to the stitching and the pockets. Every bag is lined in deadstock fabric, so linings can differ from one bag to the next.

The Parker Wristlet Wallet has 16 card slots, two exterior zip pockets and a retractable strap, so it works as a wristlet or a wallet. The Chloe Convertible Backpack and Crossbody Bag can be carried 4 ways: crossbody, sling, backpack or by the top handles. The Emma Card Holder is small but sturdy and slips into an interior pocket of a Sapahn bag.

These are our buttery soft leather handbags, made with care, the kind you will reach for every day.

The Ada Crossbody is a small to medium leather bag. Wear it as a belt bag, over the shoulder or across the body. It fits a large smartphone and a slim wallet. The Grace Crossbody is a mini leather bag you can wear as a crossbody, a clutch or a belt bag. The Ciara Crossbody and Wallet has 9 credit card slots, a clear ID window and a zippered pocket for your phone. If you are after a wallet on its own, look in Wallets.
