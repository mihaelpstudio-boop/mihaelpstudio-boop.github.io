Cleanse

/collections/cleanse

Warm Sand and Mermaid Kisses each come as a liquid soap and as a body wash, so the sink and the shower can match. Warm Sand is jasmine, mandarin and sea spray. Mermaid Kisses is golden amber and musk with a hint of citrus, plus lily of the valley, pink jasmine and vanilla.

Warm Sand Liquid Soap comes in a 10 ounce glass bottle and Warm Sand Body Wash in an 8 ounce recyclable plastic bottle, both crafted in Rhode Island. When a bottle runs low, Warm Sand Liquid Soap Refill holds 32 ounces. If you would rather have a bar, look in Bar Soap.

Bar Soap

/collections/soaps

We first experimented with cold-process soap making in the kitchen of our Newport, Rhode Island home. Atlantic, Coconut Bikini and Boardwalk are 5.4 ounce bars for hands and body, crafted in Rhode Island.

Atlantic Bar Soap pairs coconut oil, olive oil and shea butter with fine-grain sea salt from Maine and sea clay, and is scented with white jasmine, soft lavender and clean floral musk. Coconut Bikini Bar Soap has finely milled coconut shell and smells of creamy coconut, musk and warm vanilla. Boardwalk Bar Soap smells of warm sandalwood and creamy vanilla, with sea kelp extract. For liquid soap and body wash, see Cleanse.
