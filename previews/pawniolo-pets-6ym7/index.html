Treats

/collections/treats

Nick and Miki started Pawniolo Pets with grass-fed animals raised at their family cattle ranch in Kamuela, Hawai'i. All of our treats are preservative free, and our treat bags are made from cassava, wood pulp and beetroot, designed to break down in your home compost pile.

Beef Green Tripe is ground and dehydrated into crunchy, breakable sticks, and the dogs love it, even if it is stinky to us. The Beef Lung Stick is ground, piped out and dehydrated into a puffy, crispy treat that breaks easily for dogs and cats of any size. Venison Sticks are wild Hawai'i venison, ground muscle meat, naturally grass fed and finished.

Chews

/collections/chews

Our chews run from antler and horn to ears and hide, and whichever you pick, always supervise your pet while it chews.

The Deer Antler is one piece of Hawai'i axis deer antler, hard and long lasting, in Small, Medium, Large and X-Large, and weight and thickness vary. The Goat/Sheep Horns are sourced locally in Hawai'i, and each horn has its own size and shape. The Cow Ear is a Big Island grass fed cattle ear, dehydrated with the fur on and never bleached. Sizes and colors vary. If your dog would rather have something crunchy that breaks easily, the sticks are in Treats.
