Linen Collection

/collections/linen-collection

Everything in our linen line is made in Seattle, WA. The Crossbody Linen Market Tote is the foundation piece. It is fully lined, with a long strap to wear across your chest. It is meant to be used, laundered and taken on your adventures.

The Women's Linen Camp Shirt has cap sleeves and an under collar in Satsuma saffron. Its linen is grown in France, then woven in Belarus or China, and gets softer with wear. The Linen Travel Roll has 8 slots for makeup brushes, a toothbrush and a comb, and it is lined in cotton French terry. More French terry is in French Terry Loungewear.

School Uniforms

/collections/shop-school-uniforms

Our school uniforms are made by a local team in Seattle. We carry the Seattle area school plaids, from Campbell for St. Joseph School to Rampart for Our Lady of the Lake School. The skorts and skirts are soft, yet durable, and made for fun and focused class time.

The Plaid Skort is a skirt and a short in one, with a side seam pocket. The Plaid Kick Pleat Skirt sits between the skort and the knife pleat skirt in length. For a custom fit, the Custom Sized Plaid Knife Pleated Skirt is made to order. You include your student's waist measurement and preferred length. For the little ones, there is Baby Gifts and Clothing.
