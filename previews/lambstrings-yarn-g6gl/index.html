Fingering

/collections/fingering

This is my fingering yarn, dyed in my kitchen in Selkirk, N.Y. The Sadie Singles Colorwork Kits are designed for 2-color sock weight sweaters like Zweig and Garden Gate.

Sadie Singles Colorwork Kit #2- Pre-order pairs Petroleum with Summer Goth. Sadie Singles Colorwork Kit #6- Pre-order is Wolfsbane with Spellbound. The yarn is superwash merino wool, 400yds (365 meters) per 100g. Kits are pre-order, so please allow 2-3 weeks for processing and check your pattern's yardage before you pick a size.

For something rustic, look at Corrie-Spun Fingering. Corrie-Spun is our new custom base, created with Battenkill Fibers, our local fiber mill. It celebrates the Corriedale sheep.

SOCK YARN

/collections/sock-yarn-1

Tralala Sock is my sock yarn. It is a 4 ply fingering, 75% superwash merino wool and 25% nylon, 463yds (423 meters) per 100g. Hand wash cold and lay flat to dry.

Tralala Sock- Current Gothic color of the month is for anyone who loves Victorian Gothic styles, black clothes and hauntings. The colorway is a mystery until it arrives. You have until the 21st of each month to get that month's color, since it takes time to dye, dry and ship.

Tralala Sock Colorwork Kit #3- Pre-order pairs Thorn Bush with Lacewing for 2-color sock weight sweaters. The same colors come without the nylon as a Sadie Singles kit in Fingering.
