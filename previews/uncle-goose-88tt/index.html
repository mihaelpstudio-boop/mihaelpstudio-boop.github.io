Blocks

/collections/blocks

Wood blocks are how Uncle Goose started. In 1983 William Bultman found that no one in America seemed to make them, so he began hand-manufacturing alphabet blocks in Grand Rapids, Michigan, and we still do.

Uncle Goose Classic ABC Blocks gives you 28 basswood cubes with four full alphabets, numbers, math symbols and animal illustrations. Uncle Goose Classic ABC Blocks with Wagon adds a Michigan basswood wagon to tote, sort and store them; its wheel treads are lathed in Maine. Uncle Goose Planet Blocks is a 9 block set: all 8 planets plus Pluto, each with its number of moons. The Classic ABC sets are also in Classic Goose, and the planets and dinosaurs are in Discovery.

Cubelings

/collections/cubelings

Cubelings are sets of your favorite animals, squared. Uncle Goose Cubelings Sea Blocks has a puffer fish, dolphin, octopus and shark. Uncle Goose Cubelings Forest Blocks has an owl, skunk, deer and fox. Uncle Goose Cubelings Farm Blocks has a chick, cow, donkey and pig.

Each of these sets is four 1.75 inch cubes of Midwestern basswood with four paper environments. The blocks are printed with safe-to-touch inks and made in the USA. Ages 2+. Mix, match, moo. If four is not enough, bigger sets of the same 1.75 inch cube are in Blocks, like the 28 in the Classic ABC sets.
