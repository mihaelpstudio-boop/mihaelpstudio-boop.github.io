Collection Copy Pack

Every empty collection page on your Shopify store gets a description, a title tag and a meta description, written in your own voice. Paste-ready, within 48 hours.

I'm Mihael. I write collection copy for founder-led Shopify stores in the US: candles, home goods, pet treats, coffee, stationery, garden, kids, jewelry. The kind of store where the About page was written by the person who started it.

Many of these stores have the same gap: the product pages are written, but the collection pages show a title and a grid of products, nothing else. The description field is empty and the page title is whatever Shopify filled in. I write those fields so they sound like you, and you paste them in.

What you get

One pack covering every collection linked from your top menu. For each collection:

  • A description of 120 to 180 words, in the voice of your About page and product copy, with one or two links to related collections on your store.
  • A title tag of 60 characters or fewer.
  • A meta description of 155 characters or fewer.

Every fact in the copy comes from text already on your store. Where your store does not say something, you get a one-line [your answer here] prompt instead of an invented fact.

The pack arrives as email attachments: a .docx with one page per collection, a CSV for Shopify's Collections bulk editor, and a one-page paste sheet that starts with your theme's "Show description" toggle, so you can see the change as soon as you paste. One free revision round within 7 days of delivery.

How it goes

  1. Two pages, free, before anything else. I read your store's public pages, pick two empty collection pages from your menu and write them. They arrive in the body of my first email, no link to click. They are yours to use either way.
  2. Reply "go" or "show me". Within 24 hours you get the title tag and meta description for every collection in your menu plus three more full descriptions, free, together with the payment link for your tier. Both replies get the same set; "go" just tells me you expect to buy.

  4. Paste it in. About twenty minutes with the paste sheet. If anything does not sound like you, say so within 7 days and I revise it once, free.

Before

After
Description (158 words)

This is the whole line, every scent we pour, in the 8 oz amber glass jar. Each one is made by hand in small batches here in Portland, Maine, with soy wax and a cotton wick. You get about 45 hours of burn time, and when it is done the jar washes out and holds pens, matches or whatever you need a jar for next.

The names come from places around here. Casco Bay Morning is sea salt and driftwood, the smell of the ferry dock before the fog lifts. Monhegan Pine is balsam and cedar, green without being sharp. Blueberry Barrens is the sweet, slightly tart smell of the fields up the coast in August.

If you are not sure where to start, the three we sell the most of are at Bestsellers. If you would rather try a scent before committing to a full jar, the same wax comes in a 4 oz size at Travel Tins.

Wax melts are for the rooms where you do not want an open flame: a kid's room, an office, a rental where candles are not allowed. You break off a cube, set it in an electric or tea-light warmer, and the scent fills the room without a wick.

Questions

Is this written by AI?
Partly, and I would rather say so than have you wonder. The copy is drafted with AI tools, edited and approved by Mihael before anything is sent or delivered. Every factual line is checked against the text on your own store, and nothing goes out that I have not read.
Will this help my rankings?
I do not know, and I will not promise it. A collection page with a real description, a title tag and a meta description gives search engines and shoppers more to read than a blank one. Whether that moves a ranking depends on things neither of us controls, so I do not sell it on that.
What do you need from me?
Nothing before the first email; I work from the pages your store already publishes. After you pay, nothing either, unless the pack turns up a [your answer here] prompt you want filled, or you have a fact you would like included.
Do I have to give you access to my store?
No. I never ask for a login or a staff account. You get files and paste them yourself with the sheet I include.
Which collections count?
The collections linked from your top menu. Hidden, utility and discount collections are not counted and not written. A menu collection that already has a description gets a fresh one in the same format unless you tell me to keep yours; its title tag and meta description are written either way. You can also shorten the list, and the price follows the shorter count.
Can you do product pages too?
Yes. $149 for 10 thin product pages, or the $249 Whole Store pack, which includes 10 product pages with all your collections.
How do I pay?
By a Stripe payment link I send in my reply. Card or whatever Stripe offers you at checkout. You get a Stripe receipt by email; that receipt starts the 48 hours.
