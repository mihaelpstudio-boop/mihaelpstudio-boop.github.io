About page, the voice source

harborandwick.example/pages/about

I'm Dana, and I started Harbor & Wick in 2019 at my kitchen table in Portland, Maine, after a winter of melting down old candle ends because nothing I could buy smelled like the coast I live on. Every candle is still poured by hand, in small batches, a few dozen jars at a time. We use soy wax, cotton wicks and amber glass jars you can wash out and keep. The scents are named for places around here: Casco Bay in the morning, the pine on Monhegan, the blueberry barrens up the coast. If something is off with your order, write to me. I read every message, and I would rather fix it now than hear about it later.

Voice notes

  • First person. Dana writes as "I" and says "we" only for the shop as a whole.
  • Short declarative sentences. No exclamation marks.
  • Concrete Maine places, weather and rooms of the house instead of adjectives.
  • Says plainly what something is for and who should skip it.
  • No "luxury", "premium", "artisanal", "curated" or "handcrafted"; she says "poured by hand".

4. Wax Melts

harborandwick.example/collections/wax-melts

Description (162 words)

Wax melts are for the rooms where you do not want an open flame: a kid's room, an office, a rental where candles are not allowed. You break off a cube, set it in an electric or tea-light warmer, and the scent fills the room without a wick.

Each clamshell holds six cubes and 2.5 oz of the same soy wax we pour into the jars, in the same scents. One cube is usually enough for a small room, two for a kitchen or an open living space. When the scent fades, let the wax cool, pop it out and drop in the next cube.

Blueberry Barrens Melts and Monhegan Pine Melts are here year-round. Cranberry Bog Melts come in for fall and winter. If you like a melt and want the same scent with a flame, every one of them is also a candle at Soy Candles. We do not make warmers, but the wick trimmers and matches at Candle Care are ours.

6. Candle Care

harborandwick.example/collections/candle-care

Description (169 words)

Most candle problems come down to two things: a wick that is too long and a first burn that was too short. Trim the wick to about a quarter inch before each burn, and let the first burn go two to three hours so the melted wax reaches the edge of the jar.

These are the tools that make it easy. The Brass Wick Trimmer reaches down into the jar when the wax is low, which scissors cannot do. The Candle Snuffer puts the flame out without the smoke you get from blowing. Harbor Long Matches come in a glass bottle with a strike strip on the side, and they look fine left out on the table.

None of this is required; nail clippers and a steady breath get you most of the way. But if you burn candles often, the trimmer is the one I would buy first. The jars these tools are made for are at Soy Candles, and the Whole House Bundle at Bundles includes a trimmer.

The paste sheet

Collection Copy Pack for Harbor & Wick Candle Co.: paste sheet

Step 1, before anything else: turn the description on. Shopify admin > Online Store > Themes > Customize on your current theme. In the dropdown at the top choose Collections > Default collection. In the left-hand list click the Collection banner section and tick the Description box (Dawn 7 to 12 and many other themes label it "Show collection description"). Save. Until this is on, nothing you paste below will show on the page.

Step 2: the descriptions. Products > Collections. Open each collection below, paste its description into the Description box, save. Either paste the paragraphs from the .docx and add the links with the link button, or press the Show HTML button (<>) and paste the Body HTML cell from the CSV.

Soy Candles, Fall Scents, Gift Sets, Wax Melts, Room Sprays, Candle Care, Seasonal, Bestsellers, New Arrivals, Travel Tins, Wedding Favors, Bundles.

Step 3: title tags and meta descriptions, in one pass. Products > Collections. Tick the twelve collections, click Bulk edit, add the Page title and Meta description columns if they are not showing, and paste the Page title and Meta description columns from the CSV. Or, one at a time: open the collection, scroll to Search engine listing, Edit, paste the two fields, save.

Step 4: check. Open harborandwick.example/collections/soy-candles on the live store. The description should sit between the title and the product grid. If it does not, go back to Step 1.
