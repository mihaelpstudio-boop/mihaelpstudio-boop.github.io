How the pack is made

  1. I read what your store already says. Only public pages: your About page, your product pages, your shipping and returns policies, the collections in your top menu. Nothing behind a login, nothing you have to send me.
  2. I build a facts list. Every fact I am allowed to use, with the page it came from. Where you say your candles are poured in Ohio, that goes on the list with the URL. If a fact is not on your store, it is not on the list, and it cannot go into the copy.
  3. I note how you write. First person or "we", long or short sentences, which words you use and which you avoid. The About page is the main source. The copy is written to that, not to a template.
  4. I write each collection page from the list. 120 to 180 words: what the collection is for, who it suits, two or three real products from it named plainly, and one or two links to related collections on your store. Then a title tag of 60 characters or fewer and a meta description of 155 characters or fewer.
  5. Every line is checked, then I approve it. The copy is drafted with AI tools, edited and approved by Mihael before anything is sent or delivered. A check runs over each description to confirm that every factual phrase appears in the facts list and that no banned words crept in (superlatives like best and number one, promises about rankings, and the rest of that family). Where your store does not say something a shopper would want to know, you get a one-line [your answer here] prompt instead of a made-up fact.

The example pack shows the whole thing done for a fictional 12-collection candle store, including the facts list it was written from.

What arrives

Three files, as attachments to one email, within 48 hours of your Stripe receipt:

  • A CSV for the Collections bulk editor. Five columns: Handle, Title, Page title, Meta description, Body HTML. The first four are the labels Shopify's bulk editor uses; the fifth is the description with its paragraphs and links already in HTML, for the Description field.
  • A one-page paste sheet. The steps below, in order, with your collection names filled in.

Before that, on a "go" or "show me" reply, you get the title tag and meta description for every collection plus three more full descriptions, free, in the body of my email. Those are yours whether or not you pay.

How to paste it in

About twenty minutes for 15 collections. Do the first step first; without it, nothing you paste will show on the page.

1. Turn on the description in your theme

In Shopify admin go to Online Store > Themes and click Customize on your current theme. In the dropdown at the top of the editor choose Collections > Default collection. In the left-hand list click the Collection banner section and tick the Description box (Dawn 7 to 12 and many other themes label it Show collection description or Show description, and some name the section Collection header or Collection page). Save. In current versions of Dawn this box is on by default, so there may be nothing to change. The paste sheet covers the other themes; if yours is different, reply to the delivery email and I will tell you where it is.

2. Paste the descriptions

Go to Products > Collections, open a collection, and paste the description into the Description box. Paste the paragraphs from the .docx and add the one or two links with the link button, or click the Show HTML button (<>) and paste the Body HTML cell from the CSV, which carries the paragraphs and links already. Save. Repeat per collection.

3. Paste the title tags and meta descriptions

Two ways. One collection at a time: on the same collection page scroll to Search engine listing, click Edit, paste the title tag into Page title and the meta description into Meta description, save. Or all at once: go to Products > Collections, tick the collections, click Bulk edit, add the Page title and Meta description columns if they are not showing, and paste the CSV columns across. If your bulk editor does not show those columns, the one-at-a-time route takes under a minute each.

4. Check one page

Open one of the collections on your live store. The description should sit between the collection title and the product grid. If it does not show, go back to step 1.

If you hand a developer or a VA your store, hand them the paste sheet. There is nothing in it that needs a developer.

Six questions, answered plainly

1. Is this AI?
Partly. The copy is drafted with AI tools, edited and approved by Mihael before anything is sent or delivered. What that means in practice: the first draft of each page is produced with an AI writing tool working from your store's facts list and voice notes; a check confirms every factual phrase is on that list; then I read it, change what needs changing and approve it. The two free pages in my first email went through the same steps. If you would rather not use copy made this way, that is a fair position, and the two free pages are still yours.
2. Will this help my rankings?
I do not know, and I will not promise it. A collection page with a real description, a title tag and a meta description gives search engines and shoppers more to read than a blank one, and the meta description is the text that usually shows under your link in a search result. Whether any of that moves a ranking depends on your products, your competitors and things neither of us controls. The only claim I make is that the fields are no longer blank and the copy sounds like you.
3. What if you get a fact wrong, or my store does not say something?
A fact that is not on your store does not go into the copy. Where a shopper would expect one, the pack carries a one-line [your answer here] prompt, for example "ships in [your answer here] business days", and you fill it in or delete it. If something is still wrong, the revision round covers it, and if I got a fact wrong that was plainly on your store, the fix is on me, no clock. Answering a [your answer here] prompt is not a revision: send the answers any time within 30 days and I re-issue the files with them filled in, free.
4. Do you need access to my store?
No. I never ask for a login, a staff account or a collaborator request. I work from your public pages, and you paste the files in. If you want someone else to paste, the paste sheet is written so a VA or a friend can do it without asking you anything.
5. Which collections are counted, and what if some already have descriptions?
Only collections linked from your top menu count toward the tier. Hidden collections, utility collections your theme uses, and sale or discount collections are not counted and not written. A menu collection that already has a description gets a fresh one in the same format unless you tell me to keep yours; its title tag and meta description are written either way. You can shorten the list, and the price follows the shorter count. I show you the list before you pay.
6. Can you do product pages too?
Yes. $149 for 10 thin product pages, picked by you or by me from the ones with the least text, written the same way from your own facts. Or the $249 Whole Store pack, which is all your collections plus 10 product pages. Same files, same 48 hours, same revision round, same Stripe link flow.
